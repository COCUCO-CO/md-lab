# Decision record: matched LSD versus lisuride contact persistence in 5-HT2A

- ID: DR-005
- Date (UTC): 2026-07-27
- Status: APPROVED
- Research question: In the same inactive engineered 5-HT2A-BRIL scaffold and under the same membrane Hamiltonian, do the deposited differential ligand contacts persist across independent replicas: higher C227/L229 ECL2 contact occupancy for lisuride, and higher S239/N343 contact occupancy for LSD?
- Decision owner: local execution agent
- Reviewer: Tomas

## Context

Phase 5 requires a falsifiable question and human review of biological choices before any production. An active-versus-inactive comparison using 6WHA and 6A94 would simultaneously change ligand, receptor state, experimental method, fusion architecture and signaling partners. The matched 7WC6/7WC7 pair controls these variables more effectively.

This project is deliberately scoped to contact persistence in an engineered inactive scaffold. It will not infer psychedelic action, signaling bias, activation, efficacy or biological mechanism.

The supporting evidence and immutable-source hashes are in `state/decisions/PHASE5_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`.

## Options considered

### Option A — matched 7WC6 LSD versus 7WC7 lisuride

Evidence and advantages:

- Same publication, method and 2.60 Å resolution.
- Same deposited 376-residue engineered sequence.
- Same S162K, M164W and S372N substitutions.
- Same BRIL insertion and same inactive GPCRdb state classification.
- The common receptor backbone differs by only 0.376 Å C-alpha RMSD after alignment.
- Directly tests whether pre-existing differential ligand-contact patterns persist dynamically.

Limitations:

- It is not wild-type full-length 5-HT2A.
- BRIL replaces ICL3 and restricts conclusions about intracellular activation.
- ICL2 residues 181-187 are unresolved and require ensemble treatment.

### Option B — 6A94 inactive antagonist versus 6WHA active agonist-Gq complex

Advantage:

- Directly contrasts experimentally classified inactive and active states.

Limitations:

- Confounds state with ligand, X-ray versus cryo-EM, resolution, mutations, fusion design and the presence of mini-Gq/Gbeta/Ggamma/scFv16.
- Removing Gq would create an unvalidated isolated active receptor; retaining it makes the systems compositionally unmatched.

### Option C — one full 6WHA signaling complex

Advantage:

- Preserves the active receptor-Gq interface.

Limitations:

- No matched comparator for the proposed ligand-contact question.
- Much larger preparation and sampling burden.
- Missing modeled regions and multiple engineered partners complicate interpretation.

## Proposed decision

The following choices were approved by the reviewer before Phase 5 implementation.

### 1. `receptor_state`

Use the GPCRdb-classified inactive conformations in both 7WC6 and 7WC7. Do not claim or test receptor activation.

### 2. `pdb_or_model_source`

- LSD condition: RCSB PDB 7WC6, version retrieved 2026-07-27.
- Lisuride condition: RCSB PDB 7WC7, version retrieved 2026-07-27.
- Preserve both PDB and mmCIF originals plus full wwPDB validation reports.
- Orient both structures by least-squares alignment of common transmembrane C-alpha atoms to the OPM-oriented 7WC5 reference.
- Do not use a predicted full-length or silently refined receptor model.

### 3. `biological_construct`

Use one matched engineered construct for both conditions:

- receptor residues 74-265 and 313-401;
- modeled receptor ICL2 residues 181-187 included within the first range;
- BRIL fusion retained in place of receptor residues 266-312;
- BRIL sequence A1001-A1040, modeled GSGSG linker 1061-1065, and D1066-G1106;
- engineered substitutions S162K, M164W and S372N retained;
- standard charged termini at K74 and E401;
- all missing heavy atoms of retained standard residues rebuilt;
- alternate locations resolved by highest occupancy, with every selection recorded.

The conditions differ only in their experimental receptor coordinates and bound ligand. After common orientation, preparation settings and Hamiltonians are otherwise matched.

### 4. `retained_fusion_partners`

Retain BRIL in both systems. Remove no fusion from one condition without removing the identical fusion from the other. There are no G proteins or antibodies in either selected entry.

### 5. `ligand_identity_and_protonation`

Use the experimental stereoisomers and poses with chemical graphs from the RCSB CCD SDF files:

- 7WC6: 7LD, LSD, protonate atom N2, total formal charge `+1`.
- 7WC7: H8G, lisuride, protonate atom NAX, total formal charge `+1`.
- pH assumption: 7.4.
- Preserve the neutral CCD SDFs as immutable source records and generate separate, explicitly named protonated SDFs under the run preparation directory.
- Verify graph isomorphism and atom mapping from SDF to the deposited pose; never infer bond orders from PDB.
- Generate and retain a 2D depiction, isomeric SMILES, net charge and per-atom charge table.

Rationale: both basic sites have reported pKa values near 7.8, both deposited structures show a charge-assisted contact with D155, and the ligand basic nitrogen is 2.61/2.65 Å from D155 carboxylate oxygen in the source structures. A neutral-ligand interpretation remains an uncertainty and is not silently dismissed.

### 6. `receptor_protonation_microstates`

Use pH 7.4 and the same residue states in both ligand conditions:

- D155 (3.32x32) and all other Asp/Glu side chains deprotonated.
- Lys/Arg side chains protonated.
- H165 neutral delta-protonated (`HID`), supported by Reduce 4.10 hydrogen-bond geometry in both deposited structures.
- Modeled solvent-exposed H182 and H183 neutral epsilon-protonated (`HIE`) in every loop conformer.
- Tyr and Cys side chains neutral.
- K74 N terminus protonated and E401 C terminus deprotonated.

The preparation report must list every ionizable residue and its assigned state. Any tool recommendation that differs from this table requires an amended decision record before simulation.

### 7. `disulfide_bonds`

Retain the two unambiguous deposited disulfides present in both structures:

- C148-C227, the conserved TM3-ECL2 disulfide.
- C349-C353.

No additional disulfide may be inferred.

### 8. `missing_loop_policy`

- Do not build the missing N- or C-terminal regions.
- Build the five-residue BRIL linker GSGSG 1061-1065; this exact linker is present in SEQRES for both entries.
- Treat the seven-residue receptor ICL2 `IHHSRFN` 181-187 as an explicit uncertainty.
- Generate three deterministic, clash-free ICL2 conformers using the same restrained loop-sampling protocol and three recorded seeds.
- Use the same conformer index as a blocking factor for LSD and lisuride. The research replicas intentionally vary loop conformation while keeping sequence and Hamiltonian fixed.
- Accept conformers only by predeclared topology, chirality, bond-length, clash and membrane-placement gates; never select a conformer based on ligand-contact results.
- Report primary contact conclusions both pooled and stratified by ICL2 conformer. A result that changes sign across conformers is inconclusive.

### 9. `retained_structural_waters`

Remove all source HOH before standardized solvation:

- 7WC6 contains 2 and 7WC7 contains 7.
- None lies within 10.5 Å of its ligand.
- Retaining unequal remote water sets would add an unnecessary initial-condition difference.

Remove source MG, OLC, 1PE, PEG and CLR as crystallization/lipidic-cubic-phase components. The ligand is the only retained non-polymer source molecule.

### 10. `membrane_composition`

- Symmetric, homogeneous POPC bilayer.
- Amber Lipid21 POPC residue templates.
- TIP3P water.
- NaCl at 0.15 mol/L, with additional counterions for neutrality.
- 310 K and 1 bar.
- pH assumption 7.4.
- OPM 7WC5 orientation transferred independently to both conditions using the same atom selection.
- At least 1.5 nm minimum protein-image separation in x/y and 1.5 nm aqueous padding in z after construction.
- Semi-isotropic membrane pressure coupling and zero applied surface tension.

This composition is chosen for interpretability and continuity with validated Phase 4 mechanics, not as a claim that POPC reproduces a neuronal plasma membrane.

### 11. `force_field_family`

- Protein and BRIL: Amber ff19SB.
- Lipids: Amber Lipid21.
- Water and ions: Amber TIP3P-compatible parameters.
- Ligands: OpenFF Sage 2.2.1 with AmberTools AM1-BCC partial charges for the approved `+1` microstates.
- Nonbonded method: PME, 1.0 nm real-space cutoff, 0.0005 Ewald error tolerance.
- Constraints: bonds to hydrogen; 2 fs timestep.

Audit requirements:

- net charge must equal the approved value;
- atom mapping, stereochemistry and unusual assigned parameters must be reported;
- each force-field/template version and serialized OpenMM System hash must be recorded;
- a parameterization failure blocks the phase.

### 12. `replicate_count_and_length`

Mechanical gates remain sequential:

- `quick`: one 0.2 ns production check per ligand after short staged equilibration; no scientific interpretation.
- `teaching`: one 5 ns production check per ligand after full staged equilibration; no convergence claim.
- `research`: three 100 ns replicas per ligand, six trajectories and 600 ns aggregate, only after the separate pre-production human gate required by Phase 5.

Research seeds:

- Lisuride: `20262220`, `20262221`, `20262222`.

Seed rule: `project seed_base + phase*100 + 1000 + condition_offset + zero-based replicate`, with condition offsets 0 and 10.

Trajectory stride: 50 ps. Checkpoint stride: 1 ns. Approximate storage budget: 20 GB, to be recomputed from the actual prepared atom count before research approval. Approximate RTX 4090 runtime: 2-4 days for 600 ns, to be replaced by measured Phase 5 teaching performance before research approval.

## Pre-registered observables and falsification rule

Distances use minimum heavy-atom distance unless otherwise stated.

Primary observables, calculated independently in every replica:

1. `ECL2_contact`: fraction of frames with ligand-C227 or ligand-L229 distance at most 0.45 nm.
2. `deep_contact`: fraction of frames with ligand-S239 or ligand-N343 distance at most 0.45 nm.

Pre-registered directional hypothesis:

- median `ECL2_contact` occupancy is at least 0.20 higher for lisuride than LSD;
- median `deep_contact` occupancy is at least 0.20 higher for LSD than lisuride;
- each direction must be reproduced in all three loop-conformer blocks.

The hypothesis is not supported if either median difference is below 0.20, if either direction reverses, or if the sign depends on ICL2 conformer. A non-supporting result is reported as such, not repaired by changing cutoffs.

Secondary diagnostic observables:

- cationic ligand N to D155 carboxylate distance and occupancy at 0.35 nm;
- per-residue ligand contact fingerprints;
- ligand RMSD after binding-pocket alignment;
- receptor transmembrane C-alpha RMSD;
- DRY, PIF and NPxxY geometry as descriptive controls only;
- membrane area per lipid, thickness, temperature, box dimensions, hydration and maximum covalent-bond length.

No trajectory-length result will be described as converged without an independent convergence analysis.

## Equilibration and gate policy

Each prepared condition/conformer must pass:

1. restrained minimization of protein and ligand heavy atoms;
2. short NVT relaxation;
3. membrane NPT with strong protein/ligand restraints;
4. staged reduction of backbone, side-chain and ligand restraints;
5. unrestrained pre-production;
6. topology, checkpoint, trajectory, temperature, pressure, membrane, bond and ligand-pose sanity gates.

The exact durations and restraint constants will be placed in configuration before `quick` and will not change without an amended decision record. A NaN, constraint failure, missing parameter, impossible clash, corrupted trajectory or failed gate stops the phase.

## Implementation note after the first quick preflight

The preserved run
`outputs/phase05/phase05_quick_20260727T173849Z_nogit` stopped before membrane
construction or simulation. PDBFixer's initial missing-residue geometry
contained six modeled D-amino-acid centers in 7WC6, three in 7WC7, and a
0.07856 nm `PRO180:O`–`ILE181:CA` nonbonded overlap in 7WC6. Covalent bond
lengths remained within 0.2044 nm, so the first failure was specifically
modeled-region stereochemistry followed by an anchor clash.

The deterministic modeled-region preparation protocol is therefore recorded
as follows:

1. Build the approved ICL2 and BRIL-linker sequences with the profile loop
   seed.
2. For each modeled non-glycine residue, rigidly rotate the complete side
   chain about C-alpha to the median local L-amino-acid tetrahedral direction
   measured from the resolved residues of the same construct. This preserves
   every side-chain internal distance and does not change sequence,
   protonation or bond topology.
3. After adding the approved hydrogens, minimize only modeled residues
   181–187 and 1061–1065. Restrain every other heavy atom at
   10000 kJ mol-1 nm-2. Use the deterministic CPU platform,
   `CutoffNonPeriodic` at 1.0 nm, a 10 kJ mol-1 nm-1 minimization tolerance and
   at most 5000 iterations.
4. Reapply the unchanged predeclared chirality, maximum 0.25 nm covalent-bond,
   minimum 0.08 nm nonbonded-heavy-atom clash and membrane-placement gates.

An isolated reproduction lowered the 7WC6 local-relaxation potential energy
from 7.922e9 to 1.834e4 kJ/mol, increased the minimum modeled-region
nonbonded-heavy-atom distance from 0.07856 to 0.21891 nm, retained all nine
modeled L stereocenters, and left the maximum covalent bond at 0.20386 nm.
No validation threshold was relaxed and the failed run is not reused.

A subsequent independent preparation audit found that the eight-thread CPU
local minimizer reached slightly different valid local minima despite
`DeterministicForces=true`; this changed overlap-based solvent deletion by
5 waters for LSD and 18 for lisuride. The held run is
`phase05_quick_20260727T180640Z_nogit`. The local modeled-region minimization
therefore uses exactly one CPU thread. Membrane construction remains on the
approved deterministic eight-thread CPU platform. This is a reproducibility
repair only: the Hamiltonian, restraints, thresholds, seeds and scientific
comparison are unchanged.

## Consequences and sensitivity analysis

- Conclusions apply only to the engineered inactive 5-HT2A-BRIL constructs at the modeled conditions.
- BRIL prevents mechanistic interpretation of ICL3 or G-protein coupling.
- The three ICL2 conformers are an explicit sensitivity block, not interchangeable statistical replicas of one coordinate model.
- The neutral ligand microstates are chemically plausible minority states in bulk solution. They are not part of the primary experiment; a neutral-state sensitivity experiment would require a new approved decision record.
- Pure POPC is a controlled membrane model, not a neuronal membrane surrogate.
- Three 100 ns replicas can test persistence over that observation window but cannot establish equilibrium convergence or biological significance.
